Who We Are

Built Around
Denim.

Denim Machine is a dedicated denim brand born in 2020 under the Rathi Enterprises umbrella. From the first thread to the final stitch, everything we create is centred around the craft of denim.

We believe denim is more than fabric — it is a language of everyday life, worn across seasons, occasions and generations. Our work is guided by that belief.
