Our Identity

DENIM MACHINE

Denim Machine was established in 2020 as a specialized unit of Rathi Enterprises. From its inception, the brand has operated with one core focus — understanding denim from weave to wash, and producing pieces that stand the test of time.

We believe denim is fundamentally different from other apparel. It is an everyday staple, shaped by the wearer, elevated by subtle construction details, and defined by the quality of its indigo dye and topstitching.

Our Core Principles.
