Privacy Policy.

Denim Machine — A Unit of Rathi Enterprises  ·  Established 2020

1. Introduction

Denim Machine ("we", "our", or "the brand") operates as a specialized apparel unit of Rathi Enterprises, established in 2020. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and protect information submitted via our website and enquiry channels.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an enquiry through our contact forms, we may collect the following voluntary information:

  • Full Name and Contact Information
  • Company or Business Name
  • Email Address and Telephone Number
  • Product Inquiries, Volume Requests, and Message Details

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided through this website is used solely for genuine commercial purposes, including:

  • Responding to product enquiries and business collaboration requests
  • Providing requested catalogue, fitting, and order specifications
  • Maintaining professional business communication regarding Denim Machine products
  • Improving website performance, presentation, and security

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect the confidentiality of your submitted information. We do not sell, rent, or lease your business or personal details to unauthorized third parties.

5. Brand Identity & Intellectual Property

All trademarks, logos, denim photographs, product presentation designs, and textual content featured on this website are the proprietary assets of Denim Machine / Rathi Enterprises. Unauthorized reproduction or external redistribution is prohibited.

6. Contact & Legal Enquiries

For questions regarding our privacy standards or business terms, please contact our team via the Contact Page.
